What's in it

A whole ski day, in Minecraft.

Not a sled with a ski texture: an edge-carving ride model, lifts with real stations and timing, mountain weather, gear, shops and everything you need to build a resort.

Skiing that feels like skiing

Lean onto an edge with A D and carve. Dig in with S to scrub speed, tuck with W. Powder floats you once you're moving and leaves real tracks behind.

  • First Tracks combos for linked turns in untouched snow
  • Ollies, spins, flips and grabs, with landings you can stomp or crash
  • Corduroy, moguls, ice and slush all ride differently

Lifts that actually run

Doubles, quads, express six-packs that slow down in the stations, glass-cabin gondolas, T-bars and platters that tow you up on your skis, and magic carpets.

  • Two terminals and a cable: towers place themselves
  • Real detachable stations: chairlift housings stay compact; enclosed gondola stations now have a wider, longer roof canopy at both ends
  • LOAD HERE and WAIT HERE mats, countdown to your chair
  • Speed, spacing, type and opening hours from a control panel

Build your own resort

Name every run and give it a difficulty: green blue black

  • Trail signs, markers, rope-line mazes and orange closure fence
  • SLOW ZONE, EXPERTS ONLY and boundary signs, hazard poles, tower pads
  • Snow guns that make snow overnight, trail map boards, race timing
  • Snowy service roads you can ski across, and a Ski Patrol at the First Aid Kit

Mountain weather

Every morning rolls a new day: bluebird, powder day, snowstorm, blizzard, thundersnow, high wind, cold snap, flat light or spring slush.

  • Fresh powder piles up in storms; get out there for first tracks
  • Wind holds the lifts; cold snaps freeze the underdressed
  • On clear, cold nights the northern lights come out

Install

Two minutes, one file.

The mod pack sets up Minecraft 26.3, Fabric and Fresh Tracks in its own game folder. Your normal Minecraft and its worlds are never touched.

Get a launcher

Install Prism Launcher or the Modrinth App (both free) and sign in.

Import the pack

Download FreshTracks-1.2.2.mrpack. Prism: Add Instance → Import. Modrinth: + → Import from file.

Hit play

Launch the new Fresh Tracks instance, make a world (try World Type → Ski Mountains) and right-click with skis.

✨ The pack offers Sodium, Iris (shaders) and Distant Horizons as optional extras; untick them if your PC struggles. Want to jump straight in? Grab the Test World from the download page: a ready-made hill with a gondola, chairs, a T-bar, a lodge, a park and snow guns. Unzip it into the instance's saves folder. Hosting for friends? The server pack is on the same page.
