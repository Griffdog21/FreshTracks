New on the mountain

The villages, made for real.

Base Village and the Snowmass Mall are modeled on the real places, from the shops and restaurants down to what's on the menu.

Eat like a local

Every restaurant serves what it's known for: tableside guac and street tacos at Venga Venga, ribs at Slow Groovin' BBQ, beef stew at the Stew Pot, wood-fired pizza at il Poggio and the Limelight Lounge, sushi at Kenichi, breakfast burritos at Fuel Cafe. You get hungry skiing, so stop in.

Holidays in the village

Lit holiday trees with presents, the US, Colorado and Snowmass flags over the welcome monuments, a snowflake sculpture in the Mall, evergreen window boxes, signposts to the lifts and art on the walls inside.

Ski School and leaderboards

Seven quick lessons pay Ski Bucks on your first day. The board on the Base Village plaza shows the mountain's champions live; type /leaderboard for the full lists.

Come ride

Like the mod? Join the server.

You need Minecraft: Java Edition and about ten minutes the first time. After that it's just: wake the server, join, ski.

  1. Get a mod launcher

    Install Prism Launcher (recommended, free) or the Modrinth App, and sign in with the Microsoft account you play Minecraft on. It keeps the modded game separate, so your normal worlds are never touched.

  2. Install the Fresh Tracks pack

    Download FreshTracks-1.5.0.mrpack from the latest release.

    • Prism: Add Instance → Import → pick the file → OK.
    • Modrinth App: + (Create instance) → Import from file → pick it.

    It sets up Minecraft 26.3 and Fabric for you. Keep Simple Voice Chat ticked to talk to skiers near you; Sodium, Iris shaders and Distant Horizons are optional (untick them if your PC struggles). Launch it once to make sure it opens.

  3. Wake the server

    Open the Play page and press Wake the server. It takes about two minutes; wait until it says Online. It sleeps 15 minutes after the last skier leaves, so if it's asleep, just wake it again.

  4. Add the server and join

    In the Fresh Tracks instance: Multiplayer → Add Server, Server Address 18.227.232.192, Done, then double-click it.

  5. Register (first visit only)

    Type /register in chat. You land on the Base Village plaza with free skis, a snowboard, poles, goggles, helmet, jacket, snow pants, boots, hot cocoa, wax and a packed lunch. No password: your Minecraft account is your login.

  6. Ride

    Put the skis or board in your hotbar and right-click to click in. Stand on LOAD HERE to board a lift. Type /skibucks for your wallet and today's challenges, and follow the Ski School tips to earn your first Ski Bucks.

Trouble joining?

"Can't connect to server" or it times out

The server is asleep or still waking up. Open the Play page, press Wake, and wait for Online before joining.

"Incompatible client", "outdated" or missing mods

Your pack is out of date or you're in the wrong instance. Download the latest .mrpack from the releases page and import it again, then join from the Fresh Tracks instance, not the plain game.

I'm floating and can't do anything

New visitors start as spectators. Type /register and you're in.

My starter kit didn't show up

It needs 11 free inventory slots. Clear some space and type /register again.

I can't place or break blocks

That's on purpose: visitors can ski, ride lifts, shop and eat, but the resort itself is protected. Ask Griffdog21 if you'd like building permission.

Voice chat isn't working

Make sure Simple Voice Chat is enabled in your instance, then press V in game to open its settings and pick your microphone.

A few mountain rules

Be respectful, leave other players' gear alone, and enjoy the resort. Visitors can ski, ride lifts, shop and eat. Placing or breaking blocks requires the owner's permission.

The Snowmass world is hosted on the server; it isn't included in the mod download.
